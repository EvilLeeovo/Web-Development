Rules of the Game

  1. Each player places their ships on the grid secretly.
  2. Players take turns guessing coordinates to hit enemy ships.
  3. A "hit" is marked when the guessed coordinate contains an enemy ship.
  4. A "miss" is marked when the guessed coordinate does not contain any ship.
  5. The first player to sink all of the opponent's ships wins the game.
